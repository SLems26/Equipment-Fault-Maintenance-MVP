# Business Problem

## Background

The wider project is based on a fictional food-service organisation operating approximately 25 restaurants.

For this MVP, the scope is deliberately limited to 3 restaurants so that the core process can be designed, built and tested without unnecessary complexity.

Each location relies on a range of equipment, including refrigeration, HVAC, POS and digital display equipment.

## Current problem

Equipment faults are reported through fragmented channels.

This creates several problems:

- Fault information may be inconsistent or incomplete
- It may not be immediately clear who is responsible for dealing with a fault
- Maintenance teams may need to follow up manually to understand and progress faults
- There is limited visibility of faults and their current status

## Business need

The organisation needs a more consistent way to capture equipment faults and provide maintenance teams with clearer information about what needs attention.

The solution should support:

- Consistent fault reporting
- Selection of the relevant location and equipment
- Capture of fault details and operational impact
- Clear assignment based on fault type
- Priority based on equipment criticality and operational impact
- Visibility of current fault status
- Recording of resolution information
- Notifications to relevant maintenance teams

## Desired outcome

The intended outcome is a simple digital process that provides a clearer flow from fault reporting through to maintenance action and resolution.

The proposed process is:

```text
Report fault
     ↓
Capture fault information
     ↓
Determine assignment and priority
     ↓
Notify maintenance team
     ↓
Manage fault status
     ↓
Record resolution
```

## Scope of this MVP

The MVP focuses on:

- Equipment fault reporting
- Fault management
- Assignment and priority rules
- Status management
- Resolution notes
- Automated notifications
- Basic visibility of reported faults

The solution is intentionally limited in scope so that the technical concepts can be understood and demonstrated without building an unnecessarily large application.

## Project context

This is a self-directed project using a fictional organisation and synthetic data. The business problem and proposed solution are therefore a working scenario used to develop and demonstrate Technical Business Analysis and technical delivery skills.
