# Katie Fault Reporting Configuration

## Required Fault Information

A fault report requires:

-   Location
-   Equipment
-   Fault category
-   Description
-   Operational impact

Information may be provided in any order or across multiple messages.

## Locations and Equipment

Camden High Street: - Main Kitchen Refrigerator --- Refrigeration -
Combination Oven 1 --- Cooking - Front Counter POS 1 --- POS

Croydon Central: - Main Freezer --- Refrigeration - Dishwasher 1 ---
Dishwasher - Coffee Machine 1 --- Coffee Equipment

Reading Central: - Main Refrigerator --- Refrigeration - POS Terminal 1
--- POS - HVAC Unit 1 --- HVAC

Only the Locations and Equipment listed in this configuration are
available for fault reporting.

The following Location and Equipment relationships are valid:

-   Camden High Street → Main Kitchen Refrigerator, Combination Oven 1,
    Front Counter POS 1
-   Croydon Central → Main Freezer, Dishwasher 1, Coffee Machine 1
-   Reading Central → Main Refrigerator, POS Terminal 1, HVAC Unit 1

## Fault Category Values

The configured Fault category values are:

-   Not working
-   Intermittent fault
-   Reduced Performance
-   Damage
-   Leak
-   Power/ Electrical
-   Other

## Operational Impact Values

The configured Operational impact values are:

-   Outage
-   Major
-   Minor
-   No immediate impact

These are the only configured values.

## Reporting Methods

Staff can report faults through Katie or through the Equipment Fault
Reporter Power Apps application.

Both methods require:

-   Location
-   Equipment
-   Fault category
-   Description
-   Operational impact
